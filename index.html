A private place to rest, anywhere.

TOLPOD is a self-contained rest pod on wheels, built for malls, airports and busy public spaces. Step in and you get a massage chair that reclines into a bed, a pull-out desk, a TV, storage, pillows and blankets, and a smart minibar. On the public side it doubles as vending, advertising and an items-for-sale wall.
